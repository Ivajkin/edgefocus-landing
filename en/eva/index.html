Speak, pause, continue.

Voice availability depends on the browser and device. Your browser’s recognition service may process audio using its provider. Text input remains available when speech recognition is unsupported.

  1. Open EVA and turn on the microphone

    After signing in, you arrive in EVA. Allow microphone access when your browser asks.

  2. Ask your question

    Finalized speech is sent automatically after a pause. You do not need to press Send for each voice turn.

  3. Listen or read

    In supported browsers, EVA can read its reply aloud. Stop voice mode when you want to return to typing.

Is voice a live phone call?

No. Voice input turns speech into a message, sends finalized speech after a pause, and can read the assistant’s response aloud. It does not place a phone call.

Can I review the transcript before it is sent?

Voice mode sends finalized speech automatically after a pause. Use text input when you want to compose and review a message before sending.

Does voice work in every browser?

No. Speech recognition, microphone permissions and speech synthesis depend on your browser and device. If a voice feature is unavailable, use the text composer.

Is EVA free to start?

You can start with an EdgeFocus Free account. EVA uses your plan’s AI allowance and usage limits; paid plans provide different allowances. See the app for current plans.

Will EVA make the right decision for me?

EVA can help you explore your workspace context, but its answers can be incomplete or mistaken. Verify important information and make the final decision yourself.
