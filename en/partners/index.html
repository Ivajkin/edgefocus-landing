Before you apply

Is there a fixed referral commission?

Referral compensation is agreed in writing before the client introduction. The amount, payment conditions and timing depend on the engagement. No universal fixed commission is offered.

How does an integration get connected?

Publishing a listing does not install the integration or grant data access. Each customer authorizes the permissions it needs. Vendors provide their service, payment terms and support; EdgeFocus does not process integration payments.

What does submitting an application mean?

A received application starts a review. Partner status, brand use, roles and commercial terms require a separate agreement. An AgentHub listing is not a certification and does not guarantee buyers.
