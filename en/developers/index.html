Who can see my published integration?

The AgentHub catalog is available to signed-in EdgeFocus users. After publication, they can see your listing and vendor links. Listings do not have separate public pages for search engines.

How do I receive payment for a paid integration?

Buyers pay on your website through your external purchase link. You provide the service, payment terms and support. EdgeFocus does not process or settle the integration purchase.

Can I sell an integration on Developer Free?

Yes. Both Free and Pro support free and paid listings. Free allows up to 3 published integrations; Pro allows up to 25.

Does publishing guarantee customers or approval by EdgeFocus?

No. A listing makes your integration discoverable in the catalog. It is not an endorsement, a security certification or a guarantee of buyers.

How do I connect to EdgeFocus?

Use the EdgeFocus API, webhooks or MCP. Read the API documentation and MCP setup guide. Each customer grants the permissions their chosen integration needs.

What happens when I edit a published listing?

Saving edits returns it to draft and removes it from the catalog until you publish again. Your current plan’s publication limit applies when republishing.
