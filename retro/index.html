THE WEBSITE IS RETRO. THE PRODUCT IS REAL.

A home for your ideas. A way to move them forward.

EdgeFocus brings an inbox, tasks, project views and AI assistants into one workspace. Capture a loose thought, turn it into a next step, and keep the project around it in view.

This page is our little experiment: what would a modern productivity app look like on the personal web of 1998? Dragging a thought into order makes the idea easier to try than another feature list.

Created by Timothy Ivaikin and the EdgeFocus team.

Compare Free & paid plans ↗Visit the Trust Center ↗
Is this the actual app?

This is an interactive introduction. The demo inbox stays in this tab; the project board uses sample tasks; the assistant preview does not run AI. Create an account to use EdgeFocus with your own work.

Where does my demo text go?

Inbox entries stay in this tab. Your guestbook note is saved in this browser only. Neither is sent by this page. Optional analytics sends fixed events to PostHog, with no notes, page recording or saved identity. The provider still receives network information such as your IP address.

Can I start free?

Yes. EdgeFocus has a Free plan, and registration does not require a card. The plans page shows current limits and paid options. Payment is a separate step.
